CITY OF RIVERTON — DEPARTMENT OF PLANNING & DEVELOPMENT
INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

TO:      Planning Division Staff
FROM:    Robert Calloway, Director of Planning
DATE:    March 4, 2023
RE:      Project Greenfield — Accelerated Review Timeline

Team,

As you know, the Meridian Development Group has submitted its application for
the Project Greenfield riverside development at the former Atwater Mill site.
The Mayor's office has asked that we move this through review on an accelerated
schedule ahead of the summer construction window.

I met with Dana Whitfield of Meridian last week to walk through the remaining
approvals. Dana has been our main point of contact and is confident the
environmental review will not be an obstacle.

Please prioritize Greenfield permitting over the Eastside Transit items for the
next six weeks. Any questions about the site's prior industrial use should be
routed directly to me — not to the environmental assessment desk.

Bob
R. Calloway
Director of Planning
