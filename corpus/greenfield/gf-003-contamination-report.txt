CITY OF RIVERTON — OFFICE OF ENVIRONMENTAL ASSESSMENT
PRELIMINARY SITE CONTAMINATION REVIEW (DRAFT)

PREPARED BY: Elena Marsh, Senior Environmental Officer
DATE:        March 15, 2023
SITE:        Former Atwater Mill, 1400 Riverside Drive (Project Greenfield)

SUMMARY OF FINDINGS

Review of historical discharge permits and three exploratory soil borings
indicates elevated concentrations of trichloroethylene (TCE) and lead in the
northern half of the parcel, consistent with the site's prior use as a textile
finishing mill through 1981.

Boring B-2 returned TCE at levels that exceed the state residential cleanup
standard. The proposed Greenfield development places residential units directly
over this area.

RECOMMENDATION: A full Phase II environmental site assessment should be required
before any residential entitlement is granted. The deliberative discussion of
