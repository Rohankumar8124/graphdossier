MEMORANDUM — FOR THE RECORD

FROM: Elena Marsh
DATE: April 3, 2023
RE:   Suppression of Atwater Mill contamination findings (Project Greenfield)

I am documenting the following in case it is later needed.

On March 15 I submitted a draft contamination review for the Atwater Mill site
finding TCE above the residential cleanup standard. On March 16, Director Robert
Calloway instructed me verbally to stop pursuing the Atwater discharge records
and reassigned that work. My draft was not included in the materials provided to
the City Council for its March 22 closed session.

I have since learned that the "independent" soil consultant retained for
Greenfield was paid for by Meridian Development Group, not the city, and that
Meridian's lobbyist Dana Whitfield asked Director Calloway to keep that
arrangement quiet. Greg Patterson, who presented to the council, also works as a
paid consultant to Meridian — a fact not disclosed in the minutes.

The public entitlement vote is scheduled for April 11. In my professional
judgment, approving residential use over the contaminated northern parcel
without a Phase II assessment endangers future residents.

I have copied this memo to my personal records and to the State Inspector
General's office.

Elena Marsh
Senior Environmental Officer
